About YLZ Menswear

A Guangzhou menswear house built on craft and consistency.

Based in Guangzhou, one of China's garment capitals, YLZ Menswear has spent more than fifteen years making men's clothing for retailers and buyers around the world.

We run our own factory and showroom, which means we control everything from fabric sourcing and cutting to stitching, finishing and packing. Whether you need ready-to-ship wholesale styles, OEM production to your spec, or a full private-label program, our team delivers garments that meet the standard your customers expect.

  • Factory-owned production and export experience
  • OEM, ODM, wholesale and private label under one roof
  • Direct sourcing from Guangzhou — competitive pricing, no middlemen
